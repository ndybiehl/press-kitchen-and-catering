About us

Welcome home, Chef James Schendel

There is nothing quite like the flavor of home—especially when that home is Billings. After a culinary journey across the Northwest, Chef James Schendel is finally back where it all began.

A Montana culinary school graduate with a background in high-end catering and restaurant management, James most recently spent a standout stint as a brewery chef in Oregon. The pull of the “406” and cooking on his own terms brought him back to his roots—alongside Muriah, holding down the front of the house.

This isn’t just a business launch—it’s a homecoming.
